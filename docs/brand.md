CORRECTION (2026-09-12). This section previously read "photography of realflowers". There are no flowers. The photography supplied by the client is aeucalyptus cut-foliage operation: field beds, the harvest, stems graded andtied into bunches, and stock held in the packing shed. The business sells cutfoliage in trade quantities to florists, decorators and wholesalers. Confirmedwith the project owner before the site was built. See PRD section 12 question 1.

Subject matter, binding for the website: photograph what the business actuallydoes. Do not stage, buy in, or generate imagery of bouquets or event work toillustrate a product the business does not sell.

Website-only additions to the type scale. The app scale stops at 32px, which iscorrect for a dense business table and too small for a page meant to be lookedat. The website adds a fluid display scale on top of it, and nothing else:

  display-2  clamp(1.875rem, 1.3rem + 2.55vw, 3.25rem) Lora 600, tracking -0.018em

Body text on the website is 16px, not the app's 14px. These live inapps/website/app/site.css and are used by no other package.

Website layout rule. Type is never set on top of a photograph. The usual way todo that is a dark scrim under the text, which is a gradient, and gradients areforbidden above. Rather than work around the rule the site takes it as thebrief: photographs are subjects, never backgrounds. Every photograph sits in abounded plate with white around it, never bleeding to the viewport edge. Thesource material is phone photography at 1200x1600 and does not survive beingstretched across a desktop monitor, so this is also the honest treatment of it.

Website content is left-aligned, never centred. Headings, body, captions andplates all hang off the same left margin; the only thing centred on the page isthe content column itself inside the viewport. A centred block with equal whiteeither side is the most static arrangement available and it is not used, not forthe lone specimen plate and not anywhere else. Asymmetry does the work instead:the hero's standfirst is indented from the headline, the two variety plates arestaggered, and the specimen plate carries its caption in the column beside it.

Website plates carry no corner radius. The brand radii (2/4/8px) are interfaceaffordances and stay on buttons and inputs; a photograph is not an affordance,and a rounded corner on one is the loudest thing on a page saying "web card".Plates on the website have an edge, the way a plate in a book does.

Website motion (revised 2026-09-23). The site is animated with GSAP +ScrollTrigger, Framer Motion and three.js, on the project owner's instruction.GSAP owns every scroll-linked effect: photographs drift against the page, ruleare drawn from the left, blocks and the four process steps arrive. Framer Motionowns the hero load sequence and the header resolving from transparent. three.jsowns one object, a procedurally built eucalyptus stem turning beside thephotograph of a real one, rendered in Rasko Green only because the palette islocked and a naturalistic silver-blue render would introduce a hue.

Binding, whatever the library: no hidden start state ever lives in thestylesheet, so a page whose JavaScript fails is still wholly readable; the herophotograph is never animated in, because it is the LCP element; transform andopacity only; prefers-reduced-motion is honoured by all three.

Superseded note, kept for the reasoning. Two gestures and no more: something rises out of a mask, and aline extends from its left end. Plus photographs settling from slightlyoversize. All of it is CSS, including the scroll-driven reveals; the site shipsno JavaScript. Entrance animations on every section are the clearest signaturethat nobody designed a page, so reveals are confined to the plates and theblocks carrying them. Headings and body copy do not animate.
